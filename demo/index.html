Verano PhishGuard demo pages

Local test fixtures. Each one imitates a phishing technique; none of them sends data anywhere. Serve this folder with python3 -m http.server 8000 from the repo root and load the extension.

  • fake-login.html: brand impersonation, form posting to a raw IP, urgency text, dead links. Expect: blocked.
  • late-login.html: login form injected 2 s after load. Expect: banner or block once the form appears.
  • seed-phrase.html: crypto-wallet recovery-phrase harvester. Expect: blocked.
  • article.html: normal article that mentions brands. Expect: no warning.

URL-level checks (homographs, "@" tricks, typosquats) can't be demonstrated from localhost. Use Test a URL on the extension's settings page instead.
